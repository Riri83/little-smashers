🔒 Mobile Lock / Kiosk Mode Guide

🍎
iOS Guided Access

Locks the device into Little Smashers and disables Home swipe & notifications.

  1. 1
    Enable Guided Access: Open Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access and turn it ON. Set a Passcode or Face ID.
  2. 2
    Shortcut (Optional): Turn on Accessibility Shortcut so you can triple-click the side button anytime.
  3. 3
    Lock Little Smashers: Return to this app, then Triple-Click the Side/Power Button (or Home button on older iPhones).
  4. 4
    Start Session: Tap Start in the top right. Swipe-to-home and status bars are now locked!
  5. 5
    To Exit: Triple-click the Side/Power button again and enter your Passcode or Face ID.
🤖
Android App Pinning

Pins Chrome/Little Smashers to your screen and disables Home & Back buttons.

  1. 1
    Enable App Pinning: Open Settings > Security / Passwords & Security > App Pinning (or Screen Pinning) and turn it ON.
  2. 2
    Open Recents: Swipe up from bottom or press the Recents (Overview) navigation button.
  3. 3
    Pin App: Tap the Little Smashers / Chrome Icon at the top of the app preview card and select Pin 📌.
  4. 4
    To Exit: Swipe up and hold (or press Back + Home together) and unlock with your phone PIN or fingerprint.
